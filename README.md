1. GAME OVERVIEW

1.1 Concept

City Confra is a social adventure and competitive multiplayer game centered around a mysterious fraternity operating within a large modern city.

Players enter the organization as new initiates and must prove themselves through missions, challenges, social interactions, alliances, rivalries, and strategic decisions.

Every player has ambitions.

Every player has something to hide.

The ultimate objective is to rise through the fraternity’s hierarchy and become its top leader.

The game combines:

* Story-driven missions
* Exploration
* Character relationships
* Competition
* Reputation
* Decision-making
* Hidden objectives
* Teamwork
* Rivalries
* Progression
* Multiplayer interaction

⸻

2. CORE GAME FANTASY

The player should feel like they are:

“Starting with nothing and building a reputation powerful enough to control the city.”

The player begins as an unknown recruit.

They have:

* Low reputation
* Limited money
* Basic clothing
* Few connections
* No authority
* No knowledge of the organization’s secrets

Through gameplay, the player gradually gains:

* Reputation
* Money
* Skills
* Allies
* Information
* Territory influence
* Leadership positions

Eventually, the player can challenge the existing leadership structure.

4. GAMEPLAY PILLARS

City Confra is built around five major gameplay pillars.

4.1 Competition

Players compete for:

* Points
* Reputation
* Money
* Positions
* Territory influence
* Leadership

4.2 Social Interaction

Players interact with:

* Friends
* Rivals
* Mentors
* Leaders
* Civilians
* Other players

Relationships can affect missions and progression.

4.3 Mystery

The player gradually discovers that the fraternity is more complicated than it initially appears.

Important information is revealed through:

* Missions
* Conversations
* Documents
* Hidden locations
* Character relationships
* Player decisions

4.4 Progression

The player starts at the bottom and gradually climbs the hierarchy.

4.5 Choice

The player’s decisions influence:

* Reputation
* Relationships
* Mission availability
* Alliances
* Rivalries
* Story progression

8. PLAYER PROGRESSION

Players progress through ranks.

Rank 1 — Recruit

Starting position.

Rank 2 — Initiate

Unlocked after completing the initiation sequence.

Rank 3 — Member

Unlocked after proving loyalty and completing missions.

Rank 4 — Senior Member

Requires significant reputation.

Rank 5 — Officer

Player receives authority over lower-ranked members.

Rank 6 — Council Member

Player becomes part of the organization’s leadership structure.

Rank 7 — Leader

The highest normal rank.

Rank 8 — Grand Leader

The ultimate objective of the main campaign.

9. INITIATION

The game begins with a cinematic introduction.

The player receives an invitation to attend a mysterious event.

The invitation contains only:

“Your first test begins tonight.”

The player arrives at a secret location.

Several other recruits are present.

A mysterious senior member explains that everyone has been selected for a reason.

The player must complete a series of challenges.

The results determine the player’s initial reputation.

13. POINT SYSTEM

Players earn points through gameplay.

* Winning challenges
* Completing missions
* Helping allies
* Discovering information
* Completing objectives

* Betrayal

14. REPUTATION SYSTEM

Reputation is one of the game’s most important systems.

Different characters react differently depending on the player’s reputation.

Low Reputation

NPCs may:

* Ignore the player
* Challenge the player
* Refuse requests

Medium Reputation

NPCs may:

* Respect the player
* Offer opportunities
* Provide information

High Reputation

NPCs may:

* Follow the player
* Offer important missions
* Request alliances
* Fear or respect the player

⸻

15. RELATIONSHIP SYSTEM

Every major character has a relationship value with the player.

Relationship levels:

* Hostile
* Suspicious
* Neutral
* Friendly
* Trusted
* Loyal

Relationships change based on player decisions.

For example:

Helping a character may increase trust.

Betraying that character may create a permanent rivalry.

⸻

16. ALLIANCE SYSTEM

Players can build alliances.

Allies can:

* Help during missions
* Provide information
* Introduce new characters
* Assist in competitions
* Defend the player

However, alliances are not always permanent.

An ally may eventually become a rival.

⸻

17. RIVAL SYSTEM

Rivals are characters competing against the player.

A rivalry can develop through:

* Competition
* Betrayal
* Conflicting decisions
* Leadership contests

Rivals can challenge the player through special missions.

⸻

18. CHARACTER SYSTEM

The game contains major recurring characters.

The Founder

A mysterious figure whose history is unknown.

The Grand Leader

The current leader of the organization.

The Mentor

A senior member who teaches the player.

The Rival

A talented recruit who wants the same position as the player.

The Strategist

A highly intelligent member who knows more about the organization than they reveal.

The Insider

A character who possesses information about the organization’s hidden history.

⸻

19. HIDDEN MOTIVES

Major characters should have individual goals.

Examples:

* One character wants leadership.
* Another wants revenge.
* Another wants money.
* Another wants to expose the organization.
* Another wants to protect their family.
* Another wants to become famous.
* Another wants to destroy the current leadership.

Players discover these motives gradually.

⸻

20. DIALOGUE SYSTEM

Dialogue choices allow the player to influence relationships.

Example:

NPC:
“You knew this meeting was supposed to stay secret.”

Player choices:

1. “I didn’t tell anyone.”
2. “Maybe someone else found out.”
3. “Why are you worried?”
4. “What are you hiding?”

Different choices can produce different reactions.

⸻

21. DECISION SYSTEM

Important decisions can create branching consequences.

Example:

The player discovers that an ally has violated an organization rule.

The player can:

Choice A — Report Them

Gain reputation with leadership.

Lose trust with the ally.

Choice B — Protect Them

Increase loyalty with the ally.

Risk punishment.

Choice C — Blackmail Them

Gain leverage.

Potentially create a dangerous enemy.

⸻

22. FAILURE SYSTEM

Depending on the mission, failure can result in:

* Lost reputation
* Lost money
* Demotion
* Rival advantage
* New story consequences

Certain major competitions can trigger a special restart or consequence system.

43. MVP — MINIMUM VIABLE PRODUCT

The first playable version should NOT attempt to build the entire city.

The MVP should contain:

* One small city area
* One player character
* Three NPCs
* One rival
* One headquarters
* One initiation mission
* One competition
* Basic dialogue
* Basic reputation
* Basic points
* Basic player movement
* Basic UI

The goal is to prove that the core gameplay is fun before building the larger world.

44. FIRST PLAYABLE MISSION

Mission: The Invitation

Objective

Reach the secret meeting location.

Gameplay

1. Player starts in their apartment.
2. A message appears.
3. Player receives the invitation.
4. Player travels across the city.
5. Player reaches the meeting location.
6. Player meets the Mentor.
7. Dialogue begins.
8. Player receives the first challenge.
9. Mission completes.

Reward

* +100 Reputation
* +50 City Cash
* Unlocks Initiation

45. DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLE

City Confra should be developed incrementally.

Do not attempt to create the entire game at once.

The development order should be:

Player → Movement → Camera → Environment → NPC → Dialogue → Mission → Progression → City → Multiplayer

Every completed system should be tested before adding the next major system.

⸻

46. LONG-TERM VISION

The long-term vision for City Confra is a persistent social world where players create their own stories through:

* Competition
* Friendship
* Rivalry
* Leadership
* Exploration
* Cooperation
* Strategic decisions

The game should eventually allow players to create unique stories rather than forcing every player through exactly the same experience.

⸻

47. PROJECT SUCCESS CRITERIA

The first prototype will be considered successful when a new player can:

1. Start the game.
2. Create or select a character.
3. Move around the environment.
4. Meet an NPC.
5. Have a conversation.
6. Receive a mission.
7. Complete the mission.
8. Earn reputation.
9. Unlock the next objective.
10. Continue playing.

⸻

48. DEVELOPMENT MISSION

The immediate development goal is simple:

Build a small playable version of City Confra before attempting the full game.

The first milestone is not a massive open-world game.

The first milestone is:

A player walking through a small city environment, meeting the Mentor, receiving the Invitation mission, and completing the first challenge.

Once that works, the world can grow.

50. FINAL PROJECT STATEMENT

City Confra is designed to become a social competitive adventure game where players rise from unknown recruits to influential leaders.

The foundation of the game is built around one question:

How far will you go to become the most powerful person in the city?

The answer should be determined by the player’s choices, skills, relationships, and actions throughout the game.

City Confra — Enter the city. Build your reputation. Choose your allies. Rise to the top.
